**Frostpeak Drift** — a top-down 2D open-world crime game in a snowy mountain resort town. Play Raka, a snowplow driver pulled into a turf war between the Whiteout Crew and the Avalanche Kings. Explore Frostpeak on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the police across icy roads, and finish six story missions — from clearing snowdrifts in a blizzard to rescuing skiers buried by an avalanche.

- A 3200×3200 resort town to explore: ski lift, frozen lake, pine forest, cabins, resort plaza
- Traffic AI and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive with icy drift physics
- Wanted level 0–5 with police cars that chase, ram and try to bust you
- Six story missions with cash rewards, plus combat (punch), pickups and a HUD with minimap
- Title / pause / game-over / victory screens, synthesized WebAudio sound (wind, engine, sirens), snow and blizzard effects, and a save in localStorage
- Pure vanilla JS canvas — no libraries, no network requests, no outside assets

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Move with WASD or arrow keys, `E` to enter or leave a car, `F` to punch, `Shift` to run, `P` to pause, `M` to mute.
